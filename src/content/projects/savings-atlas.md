<a href="https://savingsatlas.vercel.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Savings Atlas</a> is an interactive competitive landscape mapping how neobanks, traditional banks, and specialists package business savings products — remunerated accounts, instant savings, money market funds, term deposits, and wealth offerings.

Each company card breaks a product down into headline rate, mechanism, customer value, liquidity, risk, revenue model, and strategic objective, with sourced references throughout. Filtering and a side-by-side compare view make it easy to spot where each player wins.

Tools: **Claude Code**, **Vercel**
